Overview

The analysis examines hospital encounters, patient readmissions, revisit patterns, length of stay, and departmental performance to identify trends and support evidence-based healthcare decisions.

Key Insights

- The 30-day readmission rate is 33.03%, indicating a need to investigate factors associated with patients returning to the hospital.
- Readmission and revisit patterns can help identify patients who may require closer follow-up.
- Length of stay, department, discharge disposition, and patient acuity may provide useful insights into readmission risk.

Recommendations

- Strengthen post-discharge follow-up for patients at higher risk of readmission.
- Investigate departments and discharge outcomes associated with higher readmission rates.
- Use predictive analytics to identify at-risk patients and support early intervention.
- Monitor readmission rates regularly to evaluate the effectiveness of improvement strategies.
